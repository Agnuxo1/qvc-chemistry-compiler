Historical acceptance: a real source algorithm must generate the original optimized invsqrt and DQI outputs, with exact SHA256 matches, followed by full native verification. A downloader, circuit copier or encoded KMB replay does not meet this project's source gate. The gate remains open until demonstrated.

## Original invsqrt numerical function recovered

All 256 numeric coefficient triplets and the special low-L and high-H phase-readout rules have now been recovered. An independent mathematical implementation matches the original circuit's phase exactly on **all 3,133,452 legal inputs**, with one stochastic native measurement branch per input. Input restoration and clean scratch pass for every compared input. Its worst error/tolerance is 0.9993237694056958 at x=161, matching the archived official result.

This is a full-input semantic comparison, not an exact 62-qubit circuit generator or a proof for every measurement branch. A separate 88Q circuit built from the recovered coefficients and newly fitted global phase weights passes all 200,540,928 official shots. It costs 141,336.53747428907 and remains a research study; the then-selected v0.3 c3 source circuit was cheaper. Its portable source wrapper exactly reproduces its own 29480dd3 output. [Mathematical model, construction and evidence](../studies/recovered_invsqrt/README.md).
