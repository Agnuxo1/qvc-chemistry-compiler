# Review rubric

Read the delivery document and the supplied evidence. Report only actionable discrepancies with severity, precise file/line, evidence and a concrete verification or correction.

1. Numeric results and units must match executable receipts. Local indices, official scores, simulations, source generation and physical measurements must remain distinct.
2. The two packages must not be confused. Only the reference-control variant claims generators for all three tasks. No optimized public generator or prize eligibility may be inferred from a compiled artifact or a passing simulator run.
3. Reproduction commands must resolve to supplied files and explicit external inputs. Cross-platform byte-equality claims must be supported by the integrated evidence, not by the document's own assertion.
4. Preserve authorship, third-party license boundaries, training provenance and the uncertainty of public lineage claims.
5. State demonstrated achievements and value clearly. Material limitations belong in Observaciones with their correction or next experiment; do not omit limits that change interpretation or add unsupported claims.
6. A review is not a test. Identify which documents and standards were read; a missing artifact is an incomplete review. Report a single bounded review and do not claim convergence or comprehensive five-stage EGM certification.
